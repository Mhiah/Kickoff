# The rules of Kickoff, in plain words

These are the rules every league plays by. The scoring parts of this page are what the three
scoring circuits are built from, so if a rule here changes, the circuit for it has to be rebuilt
and published again as a new circuit. A circuit that is already published can never be changed.

## Rules that are the same for every sport

**Making a pick**

- One pick per player per match.
- You can change your pick as many times as you like before the match starts. The last one you
  made before kickoff is the one that counts.
- Picks are sealed. Nobody can see anyone else's pick before the match starts, including the league
  host.

**Locking**

- Picks lock at the start time written down for the match in the league.
- After that moment no pick can be added or changed.

**Opening**

- After the match starts, picks are opened so everyone can see them.
- A sealed pick that is never opened scores 0 and shows on the player's record as "not opened".
- No pick at all scores 0.

**Scoring**

- Every sport gives 0, 1 or 3 points for a match. 3 is the most anyone can get from one match.
- The bonus points only count when the winner is right. A right margin or a right number of sets
  with the wrong winner scores 0.

**Matches that do not finish**

- A match that is postponed, abandoned or ends in a walkover or a retirement is void. Nobody scores
  from it.
- If the match is played later, it is added to the league as a new match with new picks.

## Football

**What you predict:** the exact score, as home goals and away goals.

**Which score counts:** the score at the end of normal time, which is 90 minutes plus stoppage
time. Extra time and penalty shootouts do not count. A cup match that is 1-1 after 90 minutes is a
1-1 draw in Kickoff, whoever goes through.

**Points**

| Your pick | Points |
| --- | --- |
| The exact score | 3 |
| The right result (home win, draw or away win) but the wrong score | 1 |
| The wrong result | 0 |

**Examples.** The match ends 2-1 to the home team.

| You picked | Points | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2-1 | 3 | Exact score |
| 1-0 | 1 | Home win, wrong score |
| 3-1 | 1 | Home win, wrong score |
| 1-1 | 0 | You picked a draw |
| 0-2 | 0 | You picked an away win |

**The limit:** each side's goals run from 0 to 15. A side that scores more than 15 is recorded
as 15.

## Basketball

**What you predict:** the winner, and how much they win by.

**Which score counts:** the final score, including overtime. A basketball match always has a
winner.

**The margin bands**

| Band | Winning margin |
| --- | --- |
| A | 1 to 5 points |
| B | 6 to 10 points |
| C | 11 to 15 points |
| D | 16 points or more |

**Points**

| Your pick | Points |
| --- | --- |
| The right winner and the right band | 3 |
| The right winner, the wrong band | 1 |
| The wrong winner | 0 |

**Examples.** The home team wins 104-97, a margin of 7, which is band B.

| You picked | Points | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home, band B | 3 | Right winner, right band |
| Home, band A | 1 | Right winner, wrong band |
| Away, band B | 0 | Wrong winner, so the band does not count |

## Tennis

**What you predict:** the winner, and how many sets the match takes.

**The number of sets you can pick**

| Match type | Sets you can pick |
| --- | --- |
| Best of 3 | 2 or 3 |
| Best of 5 | 3, 4 or 5 |

**Points**

| Your pick | Points |
| --- | --- |
| The right winner and the right number of sets | 3 |
| The right winner, the wrong number of sets | 1 |
| The wrong winner | 0 |

**Examples.** Player A wins a best-of-3 match in 3 sets.

| You picked | Points | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player A in 3 | 3 | Right winner, right number of sets |
| Player A in 2 | 1 | Right winner, wrong number of sets |
| Player B in 3 | 0 | Wrong winner, so the sets do not count |

## The table

Players are ranked by:

1. Total points.
2. If level, the player with more 3-point picks.
3. If still level, the player with more 1-point picks.
4. If still level, they share the position.

The table is not stored anywhere. It is worked out from the opened picks and the results, so
anyone can work it out again and get the same table.

## Your record

Every pick you have ever opened stays on your record with the points it scored, the misses as
well as the hits. It cannot be edited or deleted, and it is the same record in every league you
play in.

## Results and challenges

- The league host enters the result of each match.
- For 24 hours after a result is entered, any league member can challenge it by giving the score
  they say is right.
- The host then confirms the result or corrects it. In this first version the host has the final
  say.
- A challenge is never hidden. It stays on the match page for good, with what was challenged and
  how the host answered, so a host who enters a wrong result is seen doing it.

This is the one place where Kickoff still relies on a person. A live results feed is meant to
replace host-entered results later, without changing anything else in these rules.

## What Kickoff is not

There is no betting. Players never stake money on a pick. Prizes, where a league has them, are
paid for by the brand or community running the league.
